What the badges mean

  • Visa-ready: employer is on the INZ accredited list, or the ad offers sponsorship, and nothing in the ad rules out visa holders. Only these are emailed.
  • Agency: a recruiter posted it. Ask who the employer is and whether they are AEWV accredited.
  • Not on public list: employers can hide from the INZ list, so ask before ruling it out.
